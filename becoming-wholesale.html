Why It Works for Booksellers

Offer readers something they can't get through IngramSpark.

Direct wholesale copies are $13 each. Using the $19.99 standard paperback retail price as a benchmark, that leaves a potential gross return of $6.99 per copy before shipping and other store expenses.

Every direct wholesale copy is personally hand-signed by A.J. Beckett at no additional wholesale charge. Each copy also includes a custom Harlow University bookmark and sticker while supplies last.
