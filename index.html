About Becoming

Becoming

Harlow University. Fall 1996.

Shelby arrives on campus ready to become someone new. After years of being careful, obedient, and smaller than she wanted to be, college feels like her first real chance to breathe.

Tanner has no room for distraction. He has built his life around discipline, ambition, and a future he has worked too hard to risk.

Then they meet.

What begins as curiosity becomes attraction. What should be easy to ignore becomes impossible to stop noticing. And as the pull between them deepens, Shelby and Tanner both find themselves changing in ways neither of them planned.

Becoming is a 1996 college slow-burn romance about first-love energy, emotional awakening, mutual pining, found family, and the terrifying comfort of being truly seen. Emotionally intimate and open-door, it's a story about wanting more, becoming more, and the kind of connection that changes the shape of everything.

About A.J. Beckett

A.J. Beckett writes contemporary romance where emotion lives in both the body and the mind, where silence carries as much meaning as dialogue, and where characters become more fully themselves through the people they cannot ignore.

The work is warm, restrained, and deeply character-driven. It trusts tension, physical awareness, and the ordinary moments that change everything.

A.J. Beckett lives and writes in Northern Alabama.
