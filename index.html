About Becoming

Becoming

Fall 1996
Columbia, South Carolina
Harlow University

Shelby spent eighteen years under the roof of a mother who believed love meant correction. Stand up straight. Be proper. Smile more. Speak less.

College is her first chance to find out who she is without someone adjusting her.

Tanner has a plan for everything. Pre-law track, swim team captain, a future built on discipline and focus. He’s spent years making sure nothing knocks him off course.

The connection between them is immediate and terrifying in completely different ways.

Shelby has spent her life wondering whether she’s worthy of being loved. Tanner has spent his life avoiding anything that might derail the future he’s worked so hard to build.
Walking away should be easy. It isn’t.

Becoming is a dual-POV story of first love, finding yourself, emotional intimacy, unforgettable friendships, sharp banter, late-night adventures, and the kind of slow-burn tension that earns every single page.

About A.J. Beckett

A.J. Beckett writes contemporary romance where emotion lives in both the body and the mind, where silence carries as much meaning as dialogue, and where characters become more fully themselves through the people they cannot ignore.

The work is warm, restrained, and deeply character-driven. It trusts tension, physical awareness, and the ordinary moments that change everything.

A.J. Beckett lives and writes in Northern Alabama.
